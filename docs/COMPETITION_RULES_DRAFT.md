## 1. The promoter
The promoter is Evolution Golf, Ringwood, Hampshire. [LEGAL REVIEW: insert registered company name, number and address.]

## 2. Eligibility
- Entrants must be aged 18 or over. [LEGAL REVIEW: confirm minimum age and any junior format.]
- Employees of the promoter and their immediate families may enter but are not eligible for prizes. [BUSINESS DECISION]
- Entrants must attend the Evolution Golf store in person. Remote or proxy entries are not accepted.

## 3. How to enter
- Each entry is one round of the number of shots published for that week's competition (normally five).
- Rounds are played on Evolution Golf's simulator under staff supervision, at a booked session or as a walk-in when the simulator is available.
- The number of rounds a player may enter in one competition may be limited, as published for that competition.
- A free method of entry, where offered, is described on the competition page. [LEGAL REVIEW: confirm whether a free entry route is required for the chosen format.]

## 4. The challenge and scoring
- The featured hole, simulator settings and course conditions are published for each competition and remain fixed for its duration.
- Each shot's distance to the hole is taken from the simulator display and recorded by staff in millimetres; results are displayed in metres.
- Unless the competition page states otherwise, a shot only counts if the ball finishes on the green.
- A player's leaderboard position is decided by their single closest valid shot across all of their rounds in that competition.
- Staff may declare a shot invalid (for example a practice swing, a ball struck out of turn, or a simulator mis-read) and will record the reason.
- Rounds can be started up to the published closing time. A round started before the closing time may be completed after it.

## 5. Ties
- The tie procedure is published on each competition page before it opens and will not be changed after it opens.
- Default procedure: players with exactly equal closest shots share the position and share the combined prize(s) for the positions they occupy equally.
- A verified hole-in-one is recorded as a distance of 0.00 m. Multiple verified hole-in-ones tie with each other; an earlier hole-in-one does not outrank a later one.

## 6. Hole-in-one jackpot
- Where a jackpot is advertised, a hole-in-one must be witnessed by a member of staff, supported by simulator evidence (screenshot), and approved by a manager who did not witness it.
- The number of jackpot payouts, the per-player limit and the total maximum payout are shown on the competition page. Once the maximum is reached, further hole-in-ones are recognised but do not receive the jackpot.
- [LEGAL REVIEW / INSURANCE: confirm the jackpot is funded or insured and that the cover applies to simulator play.]

## 7. Verification, disputes and results
- Results are provisional until verified by a manager after the competition closes.
- Any dispute must be raised in store or by email before results are finalised. The promoter's decision, made in good faith on the recorded evidence, is final. [LEGAL REVIEW]
- The promoter may disqualify any entry it reasonably believes breaches these rules.

## 8. Prizes
- Prizes are as published for each competition. There is no cash alternative to non-cash prizes.
- Winners are notified by email and must claim within the published claim period (normally 30 days), after which the prize may lapse. [LEGAL REVIEW]
- Winners may be asked to provide photo ID matching their entry details.
- [LEGAL REVIEW: tax, payment method and timing of prize payments.]

## 9. Publicity
- The leaderboard shows your first name and surname initial (e.g. "James R."). You can choose an anonymous display name in your account.
- Full names or photographs are only published with your separate consent.

## 10. Changes and cancellation
- Material changes to a competition after it opens (for example extending the deadline or increasing a prize) are logged and notified to entrants. Prizes will not be reduced and scoring or tie rules will not be changed after a competition opens.
- If a competition has to be cancelled, unplayed paid entries will be refunded. [LEGAL REVIEW: refunds for played rounds in a cancelled competition.]

## 11. Data protection
Personal data is processed in accordance with the Pin Hunters privacy notice.
